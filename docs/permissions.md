# Data permissions

Some word lists bundled with lexinspect have no open licence. Where the authors were asked for permission, their answers are recorded here. Licences for every bundled file are in [`src/lexinspect/data/LICENSE.md`](../src/lexinspect/data/LICENSE.md), and sources in [`SOURCES.md`](../src/lexinspect/data/SOURCES.md).

## PHRASE List (Martinez & Schmitt 2012)

Norbert Schmitt's resources page publishes the list with no terms of use, so both authors were asked by email.

| Date | From | Asked about | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-16 | Norbert Schmitt | The hosted app, and `phrase.json` with its added variant fields in the public repository | "I am fine with this, but the list is really Dr. Martinez's work." He cc'd Ron Martinez. |
| 2026-10-30 | Ron Martinez | The app, the open-source repository, and "the study materials based on the list", with full attribution to both authors | "As long as Norbert is also OK with it, I can also give the green light." |

Neither author attached conditions beyond attribution. Schmitt called the list Martinez's work, and Martinez gave the permission. Martinez's one condition was Schmitt's agreement, which was on record two weeks before. The emails are kept by the maintainer.

What this covers: free, non-commercial use of the list in lexinspect, including the hosted app and `phrase.json` in this repository, with full attribution to both authors (Martinez & Schmitt 2012). It does not cover commercial use or reuse of the list outside lexinspect. Ask the authors for those.
