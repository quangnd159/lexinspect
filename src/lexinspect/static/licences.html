lexinspect

data sources

lexinspect analyses text against published vocabulary lists compiled by other researchers. Those lists are their work, not ours. Several are shared under licences that require attribution to you, the person receiving the analysis — not merely to whoever reads the source code. This page is that attribution. If you publish anything derived from lexinspect output, cite the original authors below, not lexinspect.

Academic Word List — awl

Coxhead, A. (2000). A new academic word list. TESOL Quarterly, 34(2), 213–238.
570 head words across 10 sublists. Freely usable for educational and research purposes.

BNC/COCA word-family lists — bnc/coca

Nation, P. BNC/COCA word-family lists (1k–10k head words).
Via the lpmi-13/machine_readable_wordlists mirror. Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal.

New General Service List and New Academic Word List — ngsl

Browne, C., Culligan, B., & Phillips, J. (2013). The New General Service List 1.2 and The New Academic Word List 1.2.
Source: newgeneralservicelist.com. Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 — attribution and share-alike required.

CEFR vocabulary profiles — cefr

CEFR-J Vocabulary Profile 1.5 (A1–B2). Compiled by Yukio Tono, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies. Copyright Tono Laboratory, TUFS. Free for research and commercial use with citation; this page is that citation.
Octanove Vocabulary Profile C1/C2 1.0. Octanove Labs. Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0.
Both via openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj.

Not the Cambridge English Vocabulary Profile. Different corpora and methodology; results will diverge from EVP-based tools.

Metadiscourse markers — metadiscourse

Hyland, K. (2005). Metadiscourse: Exploring Interaction in Writing. Continuum, London and New York.
The ten-category taxonomy is Hyland's intellectual contribution. Cite Hyland (2005) in any output.

Phrasal Expressions List — phrase

Martinez, R., & Schmitt, N. (2012). A phrasal expressions list. Applied Linguistics, 33(3), 299–320.
Via Norbert Schmitt's resources page, which states the materials are freely available for research and pedagogical purposes. lexinspect is offered free of charge for exactly those purposes.

Hunspell dictionaries — errors

LibreOffice English dictionaries (en_GB, en_US). Wordlist by Kevin Atkinson (LGPL); affix file by Geoff Kuenning (BSD). Via LibreOffice/dictionaries, pinned to a fixed commit.

Software

lexinspect itself is MIT licensed. It stands on spaCy, textstat, lexicalrichness, spylls, and FastAPI.

The authoritative, versioned copy of this list — including provenance notes and known trade-offs for each dataset — lives in data/SOURCES.md.
