Privacy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

lexinspect is a free tool run by Quang Nguyen. This page explains what happens to the text you analyse and to anything else the site handles. In short: your text is analysed and then discarded. lexinspect has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Your text

  • When you press Analyse, your text is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to lexinspect's server. It is analysed in memory and sent back to your browser with the results. The same applies to text sent to the API.
  • lexinspect's code doesn't store, log or share your text, and doesn't use it to train anything. The analysis runs once, and the text is gone when the response is sent.
  • Your browser keeps your current draft in its own local storage, so it survives a page reload. It stays on your device. You can clear it by clearing the text box or your browser's data for this site.

Who else is involved

lexinspect relies on two companies to run the site. Each one handles a limited amount of data under its own privacy terms.

The analysis runs in the United States (Washington, D.C.). Pages are served from the nearest Vercel location.

Fonts are served from lexinspect's own site, so loading a page sends nothing to a font service. The one exception is the API documentation page (/docs), which loads its viewer from the jsDelivr CDN. That page never handles your text.

For schools and institutions

  • Students' writing. Don't paste anything that identifies a student, such as a name or an ID number. lexinspect doesn't need it to analyse a text.
  • Data protection. lexinspect is offered as is, with no contract or data processing agreement. If your institution needs one, or needs data to stay in a particular country, run lexinspect yourself. It's open source, installs with one command and works entirely offline: see the README.
  • Children. lexinspect has no accounts and asks for no personal information. In class, use it under a teacher's supervision.

Your rights

lexinspect doesn't keep your text or build a profile of you, so there is normally nothing to access, correct or delete. For anything Vercel or Cloudflare holds, see their policies above. Questions: quangnd159@gmail.com.
