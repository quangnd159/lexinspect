See what makes a text hard, in the text itself.

lexinspect profiles English writing the way applied linguists do: readability by eight published formulas, vocabulary against CEFR-J, Nation’s BNC/COCA families, NGSL and the AWL, multiword expressions from Martinez & Schmitt’s PHRASE List, sentence complexity after Lu’s L2SCA, Hyland’s metadiscourse and a UK/US spell check. Every finding is marked where it occurs, with what to change for readers at a given CEFR level.

  • Free and open. No account, nothing stored. Your text is analysed and forgotten.
  • Cited, not guessed. Every list and formula is the published one, with its source and licence on the Sources page.
  • Open source. Run the same analysis yourself with the lexinspect command-line tool or Python package.
